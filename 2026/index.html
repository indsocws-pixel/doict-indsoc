DOICT-IndSoc 2026

Call for Papers

The inaugural DOICT-IndSoc Workshop focused on integrating data, operation, information, and communication technologies for Industry 4.0 and Society 5.0. The event brought together research in industrial communication networks, cyber-physical systems, artificial intelligence, cloud and edge computing, blockchain, digital twins, and intelligent automation.

Its motivation centered on using the convergence of technologies to build trustworthy, adaptive, efficient, and interoperable industrial and societal systems.

Topics of Interest (2026)

  • Architectures for DOICT fusion in Industry 4.0 and Society 5.0
  • Communication networks for industrial automation and cyber-physical systems
  • Service-based architectures and sustainable technologies for Industry 4.0
  • Cyber-physical integration and human-centric intelligence for Society 5.0
  • Cross-domain and multi-resource management for industrial applications
  • Edge intelligence and information security for Industry 4.0 and Society 5.0
  • Network support for human–AI agent–device collaboration and cooperative autonomy
  • Blockchain-based trust, supply chains, and decentralized identity management
  • Payment channel networks and incentive design for multi-party collaboration
  • Software and data compliance and assurance in Industry 4.0
  • AI-model-empowered sequence planning for manufacturing in Industry 4.0
  • Time-series forecasting for operation and maintenance in Industry 4.0
  • V2X and 6G networks for connected mobility in Society 5.0
  • Intelligent transportation systems for smart cities in Society 5.0
  • AI model verification and interpretability for industrial intelligence
  • AI model privacy and data confidentiality protection in industrial environments
  • Digital-twin-driven resource management and task scheduling in smart manufacturing
  • Human–AI agent–device collaboration and cooperative autonomy
  • Connected robots, devices, vehicles, and industrial IoT platforms
