Scope of the inaugural edition

The first DOICT-IndSoc Workshop explored the convergence of Data Technology (DT), Operational Technology (OT), Information Technology (IT) and Communication Technology (CT), in the context of Industry 4.0 and Society 5.0.

The workshop brought together research on reliable industrial networks, cyber-physical systems, edge and cloud intelligence, digital twins, trustworthy AI and decentralized collaboration. Topics spanned industrial automation and smart manufacturing to networked mobility and human–AI cooperation. The organizers reported participation in the submission process from 20 countries and regions.
