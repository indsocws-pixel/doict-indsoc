The Inaugural Workshop

The first DOICT-IndSoc Workshop took place on May 18, 2026 in Tokyo, Japan, alongside IEEE INFOCOM 2026. The workshop explored integration of data, operational, information and communication technologies for Industry 4.0 and Society 5.0.

The organizers reported that the inaugural edition attracted submissions from 20 countries and regions.
