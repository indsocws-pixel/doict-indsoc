DOICT-IndSoc 2027

Call for Papers

Industry and society are entering a new era of intelligent, connected, and data-driven systems. The convergence of data, operational technology, information technology, and communication technologies offers new opportunities for industrial digitalization, networked intelligence, and human-centered services.

The 2nd DOICT-IndSoc Workshop, held in conjunction with IEEE INFOCOM 2027, invites researchers and practitioners to share original research on the deep integration of these technologies in the Industrial Internet and Society 5.0. Emphasis is placed on networking and communications, cyber-physical systems, industrial transformation, and emerging cross-disciplinary applications.

Following the inaugural workshop, which attracted submissions from 20 countries and regions according to the organizers' announcement, DOICT-IndSoc continues to promote academic exchange and industry–academia collaboration.

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning for industrial and societal systems
  • Blockchain, security, privacy, and trusted industrial applications
  • Digital twins, modeling, and intelligent cyber-physical systems
  • Edge computing, edge intelligence, and distributed inference
  • Industrial Internet, networking, and communication systems
  • Industrial control, automation, and digital transformation
  • Human–machine interaction, collaboration, and intelligent systems
  • Integration of data, operation, information, and communication technologies
  • Intelligent transportation, connected mobility, and smart cities
  • Cross-domain industrial applications and emerging architectures
