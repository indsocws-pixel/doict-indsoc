Workshop overview

Connecting intelligent industry and society

The 2nd DOICT-IndSoc Workshop at IEEE INFOCOM 2027

Industrial Internet and Society 5.0 rely on reliable communication networks, distributed intelligence, and seamless coordination between physical and digital systems.

The fusion of Data Technology (DT), Operational Technology (OT), Information Technology (IT) and Communication Technology (CT)—collectively referred to as DOICT—enables predictive and adaptive industrial systems. This convergence brings challenges in network determinism, ultra-reliability, real-time response, cross-domain resource management and secure collaboration, alongside growing requirements for verifiable, explainable and privacy-preserving AI.

Building on its inaugural edition, which attracted submissions from 20 countries and regions, DOICT-IndSoc 2027 brings together researchers, practitioners and industry experts to explore DOICT convergence for intelligent industrial networks and human-centered services.

Research themes

Topics of Interest

We invite original research on the convergence of DOICT in industrial systems and Society 5.0. Topics include, but are not limited to:

Architectures for DOICT fusion in Industrial Internet
Communication networks for industrial automation and cyber-physical systems
Service-based architectures and sustainable technologies for Industrial Internet
Cross-domain and multi-resource management for industrial applications
Edge intelligence and information security for Industrial Internet
Network support for Human–AI agent–device collaboration and cooperative autonomy
Blockchain-based trust, supply chain, and decentralized identity management
Connected robots, devices, vehicles, and industrial IoT platforms
Payment channel networks and incentive design for multi-party collaboration
Software and data compliance and assurance in Industrial Internet
Time-series forecasting for operation and maintenance in Industrial Internet
AI model verification and interpretability for industrial intelligence
AI model privacy and data confidentiality protection in industrial environments
Digital twin-driven resource management and task scheduling in smart manufacturing
Human–AI agent–device collaboration and cooperative autonomy
Related autonomous driving technologies in Intelligent Transportation System
AR/VR applications and related technologies in Industrial Internet and Society 5.0
