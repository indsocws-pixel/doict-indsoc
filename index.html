About the workshop

Connecting intelligent industry and Society 5.0

The second DOICT-IndSoc Workshop brings together researchers and practitioners exploring the deep integration of data, operation, information, and communication technologies in the Industrial Internet and Society 5.0.

The workshop emphasizes cross-disciplinary advances in networking and communications, cyber-physical systems, and industrial digital transformation. It provides a forum for new architectures, systems, applications, and collaborative research spanning academia and industry.
